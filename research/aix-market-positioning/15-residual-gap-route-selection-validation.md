## 2. Scope and non-goals

Stage15 stops at Strategy + Capability Level. It may define route-level capability domains and evidence requirements, but it does not authorize a build or an implementation path.

The stage does not:

- start with a capability backlog and work backward to a route rationale;
- prioritize Remittance, QR, Send, card, wallet, or other features;
- define feature behavior, UX, pages, APIs, fields, state machines, error handling, instrumentation, schema, or implementation;
- select a vendor, partner, owner, sponsor, commercial route, or regulatory operating model;
- select a launch market; or
- use AIX internal data in a Stage15 route decision.

Rail and feature references are allowed only as mechanisms or current alternatives inside an outcome-level comparison. They are not routes by themselves.

## 4. Minimum validation unit and cell integrity

The minimum validation unit is:

**Route × Country × Cohort × Job/Outcome × Current Alternative Stack**

Every claim, evidence card, gate status, and adjudication must identify one such cell. A valid Gate PASS requires a complete evidence chain inside the same country + cohort + outcome cell, including:

1. the exact route and country;
2. the cohort definition and denominator;
3. the same Job/Outcome;
4. the starting-money context;
5. the Current Alternative Stack and outcome-level coverage;
6. observed behavior, frequency/value signal, and retain/hold/use motive where relevant;
7. residual pain plus a switching or parallel-use reason where relevant;
8. relationship-depth interpretation;
9. research reachability; and
10. source, date, support-or-disconfirm status, and limitations.

Evidence cannot be stitched across countries, cohorts, or outcomes. For example, an Argentina motivation signal, a Philippines rail gap, and a global survey cannot become an H1 Gate PASS. They may remain separate context records, but they do not form one matched cell.

Missing, inaccessible, non-comparable, or incomplete evidence is **NOT_YET_PASS** and only blocks promotion. It is not a kill signal. A cell may be marked **FALSIFIED_IN_CELL** only when direct disconfirming evidence or a satisfied pre-registered falsifier is recorded in that same cell.

## 8. Decision-gate protocol

All gates start at **NOT_YET_PASS** for H1 and H2. A candidate is not promoted unless its required matched evidence clears DR-1 through DR-5 and survives the H0/Current Alternative Stack comparison. No numerical threshold is invented in this architecture.

### DR-1 Job/Target

- **Initial status:** NOT_YET_PASS.
- **Pass evidence:** Direct evidence in the same Route × Country × Cohort × Job/Outcome × Current Alternative Stack cell shows that the target cohort repeatedly or at high value experiences the route-specific Job/Outcome, with its starting-money context and denominator.
- **Falsifier:** Direct same-cell evidence shows that the Job is sparse, occasional, low-value, or mainly not a digital-dollar/stablecoin relationship.
- **Decision if failed:** If the falsifier is directly observed, mark the cell FALSIFIED_IN_CELL and block promotion from that cell; route-level elimination requires the registered adjudication, not a missing sample. If evidence is missing or non-comparable, remain NOT_YET_PASS.

### DR-2 Differentiated outcome

- **Initial status:** NOT_YET_PASS.
- **Pass evidence:** The same country + cohort + outcome comparison covers the Current Alternative Stack and shows material residual pain plus a reason to switch to or use AIX in parallel.
- **Falsifier:** Direct same-cell comparison shows existing alternatives sufficiently solve the outcome and no meaningful residual gap remains.
- **Decision if failed:** A direct falsifier produces FALSIFIED_IN_CELL and prevents Primary promotion from that cell; matched alternative sufficiency may support H0 / NO PRIMARY SELECTED. Do not repackage the same feature or rail bundle as a new route. Missing evidence remains NOT_YET_PASS.

### DR-3 Defensibility

- **Initial status:** NOT_YET_PASS.
- **Pass evidence:** The candidate difference is grounded in relationship or outcome depth that is difficult to simple-fast-follow and is not explained only by having more rails, cards, or features.
- **Falsifier:** Direct comparison shows a simple rail/feature bundle can close the outcome and no independent continuity, local depth, or retained relationship exists.
- **Decision if failed:** A direct falsifier produces FALSIFIED_IN_CELL and the candidate cannot become Primary from that cell; no route-level moat is inferred. Missing or weak evidence remains NOT_YET_PASS and does not itself falsify.

### DR-4 Reachable geography

- **Initial status:** NOT_YET_PASS.
- **Pass evidence:** At least one of the three pre-assigned market roles has a reachable same-country cohort and a complete same-cell Job/Outcome plus residual-gap chain.
- **Falsifier:** The pre-registered coverage plan completes and no assigned role market has an identifiable reachable cohort with the required same-cell Job/Outcome and residual gap.
- **Decision if failed:** Only the completed, direct geography falsifier can produce FALSIFIED_IN_CELL and eliminate the geography-based route hypothesis; lack of access, an absent source, or incomplete coverage remains NOT_YET_PASS. No launch market is selected.

### DR-5 Money relationship depth

- **Initial status:** NOT_YET_PASS.
- **Pass evidence:** Direct same-cell behavior shows repeated receive → retain/manage → local reuse for H2, or repeated digital-dollar → local-use continuity with meaningful everyday depth for H1.
- **Falsifier:** Direct same-cell behavior is mainly immediate cash-out, one-off spend, or transport-only delivery, with no repeated retained or local-use relationship.
- **Decision if failed:** A direct falsifier produces FALSIFIED_IN_CELL and downgrades the cell to utility/transport evidence rather than a Primary money relationship; missing evidence remains NOT_YET_PASS and cannot kill the candidate.

## 9. Decision protocol and end states

1. Evaluate H1 and H2 separately. H3 remains a dimension and cannot be promoted independently.
2. Record candidate-supporting, competitive disconfirmation, disconfirming/falsifier-triggered, and insufficient evidence separately.
3. Apply each gate within the same country + cohort + outcome cell. Do not infer a PASS from cross-market stitching or a favorable narrative.
4. Treat H0 as the winner only when the matched Current Alternative Stack sufficiently solves the outcome or a candidate's registered falsifier is directly triggered. Insufficiency alone does not create a falsifier.
5. If both H1 and H2 appear to clear all gates, perform a same-level comparative decision before selecting one Primary. There is no automatic co-Primary.
6. Route promotion, if any, is limited to the two candidate end states below; it does not change P1, P2, the roadmap, or the Strategy + Capability Level scope.
