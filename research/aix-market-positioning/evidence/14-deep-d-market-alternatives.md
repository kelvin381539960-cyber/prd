# Stage14 Deep Dive D — Market alternatives and right-to-win pressure

> **Archive status:** Controller-verified evidence materialization; bounded package only.
>
> **Evidence class:** Provider-reported competitor scale, product propositions, and operating benchmarks. These are alternative pressure signals, not an independent stablecoin market size or retention proof.

## Question and scope

This deep dive records credible alternatives that can cover some or all of the receive → hold → spend job. It is intended to discipline AIX positioning: a stablecoin rail alone is not a right-to-win when fiat cross-border products, stablecoin-native wallets, and payroll-linked wallets already offer adjacent capabilities.

The machine-readable companion is [14-market-alternatives-data.csv](data/14-market-alternatives-data.csv). The cited competitor pages remain link-only in [MANIFEST.csv](source-files/14/MANIFEST.csv); no gated competitor report or arbitrary HTML page is archived.

## Evidence ledger

### Wise — mature fiat alternative

Wise FY26 reports 18.9 million active customers, $243.5B of cross-border volume, $39B of holdings, $43.6B of card spend, and 75% of Q4 payments under 20 seconds. These figures show a mature fiat receive/hold/spend and speed alternative. They do not measure stablecoin behavior or a stablecoin target population.

### KAST — direct digital-dollar proposition

KAST reports more than 1 million users and close to $5B of annualized transaction volume. Its freelancer proposition describes earning globally, holding digitally, and spending locally; the freelancer route includes receiving USD from a client, Stripe, or Deel before holding digital dollars and spending. This is direct competitive pressure for the same job, but it is provider-reported proposition and scale evidence, not an independently measured cohort or retention result.

### RedotPay — generic crypto/stablecoin spend pressure

RedotPay reports more than 8.5 million users in 100 countries and approximately $14B in annual payment volume. This is meaningful generic crypto/stablecoin spend-rail pressure. It does not establish unique stablecoin users, retained balances, target-country adoption, or AIX right-to-win. The source is retained as a link-only entry; no downloaded RedotPay archive is required.

### Deel — payroll-linked wallet alternative

Deel reports a stablecoin wallet live in 80+ countries, launched first in Argentina about one month earlier, and explicitly moves the contractor relationship from getting paid toward holding a dollar-backed balance and spending. This is competitive and same-job market validation. It is not independent market size, a linked same-user cohort, or a measurement of retained balances.

## Interpretation boundary

The alternatives show that a target worker or cross-border user may already be able to receive, hold, and spend through several product shapes: mature fiat infrastructure, a digital-dollar wallet, a generic crypto card, or a payroll-linked wallet. The evidence does not show how much user overlap exists or which product wins on primary job, cost, trust, compliance, speed, or retention.

Provider-reported user counts and payment volumes are not additive and should not be used to estimate a market. Their strategic value here is competitive pressure and a requirement to articulate AIX-specific differentiation.

## Decision impact

Treat AIX right-to-win as unproven. Strategy must compare AIX with the actual alternative set on the target job—receive, hold, spend, cash-out, and repeat use—not merely on whether AIX supports stablecoins. Competitor scale and propositions justify a focused differentiation and switching validation track.

## What remains Unknown

- Same-user overlap across Wise, KAST, RedotPay, Deel, and AIX.
- Which alternative is used for the primary job versus a secondary rail.
- Balance retention, spend frequency, cash-out behavior, and repeat income-cycle use.
- Country availability, pricing, FX economics, trust, compliance, and operational reliability by target segment.
- AIX-specific advantage that is both valued by the target user and difficult to copy.

## Disconfirming evidence

- Wise’s reported scale, holdings, card spend, and speed may satisfy the target job without a stablecoin product.
- KAST and Deel directly cover globally paid workers who want to hold and spend dollar value.
- RedotPay’s reported footprint and payment volume show generic crypto/stablecoin spend alternatives already have reach.
- Provider-reported counts may be promotional, non-comparable, or measured with different definitions; they cannot prove market leadership or user retention.

## Recommended validation evidence

At strategy level, compare AIX with the named alternatives through target-user switching research and matched product benchmarks. Require evidence of the primary job, current alternative, reason to switch, hold/spend outcome, repeat-cycle behavior, and a defensible AIX differentiation before claiming a right-to-win.
