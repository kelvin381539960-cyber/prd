## 2. Cell and matching rule

The minimum validation unit is:

**Route × Country × Cohort × Job/Outcome × Current Alternative Stack**

Each evidence card must identify one cell. A Gate PASS requires a complete evidence chain within the same country + cohort + outcome cell. No claim may be stitched across countries, cohorts, or outcomes.

An Argentina motivation signal, a Philippines rail gap, and a global survey are separate context records; together they cannot form an H1 PASS. Missing, inaccessible, non-comparable, or incomplete evidence is NOT_YET_PASS and only blocks promotion. Only direct disconfirming evidence or a satisfied pre-registered falsifier in the same cell can produce FALSIFIED_IN_CELL.

## 3. Route and market-role labels

Allowed route labels:

- H1 — Digital-dollar value continuity → country-specific local everyday purchasing-power depth.
- H2 — Destination-side retained digital-dollar relationship after cross-border income/remittance.
- H0 / Null — Existing alternatives already solve the outcome sufficiently / no differentiated Primary route selected.
- H3 dimension — Source-of-funds / relationship-anchor dimension; never an independently promoted route.

The three validation market roles are:

- PH hard-baseline;
- one H2 diagnostic market; and
- one strong-substitution market.

The latter two market assignments must be selected before research begins using the same pre-registered conditions: identifiable and reachable cohort, declared starting-money context, observable same-level Job/Outcome, observable Current Alternative Stack, defined denominator/source plan, and role assignment made before findings are seen. These roles are not launch-market decisions.

## 5. Evidence-status adjudication

| Status | Meaning | Decision treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate-supporting | Same-cell evidence supports the specified Job, outcome, residual gap, relationship depth, or reachability claim. | Contributes to the relevant gate; does not automatically produce PASS. |
| Competitive disconfirmation / NOT_YET_PASS | Strong incumbent or alternative pressure is present, but the record does not lock one country + cohort + outcome cell or otherwise meet the falsifier rule. | Records competitive pressure and remains NOT_YET_PASS; never produces FALSIFIED_IN_CELL. |
| Disconfirming / falsifier-triggered | Direct same-cell evidence or a pre-registered falsifier is satisfied. | May produce FALSIFIED_IN_CELL and the applicable CELL/ROUTE FALSIFIED decision. |
| Insufficient | Evidence is absent, inaccessible, non-comparable, or incomplete. | Remains NOT_YET_PASS / no promotion; never kill or downrank solely for insufficiency. |

The red-team adjudication must keep support, competitive disconfirmation, direct falsification, and insufficiency separate. A source difficulty or missing denominator is not a falsifier.
