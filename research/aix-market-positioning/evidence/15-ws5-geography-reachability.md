## 3. Same-cell reachability requirements

An assigned country can contribute to a Gate only when the package can identify and reach:

1. the exact route and country;
2. the defined cohort and starting-money context;
3. the same Job/Outcome;
4. the observable Current Alternative Stack;
5. a denominator/source plan; and
6. the residual-pain or relationship-depth evidence relevant to the route.

Country availability, product eligibility, macro pressure, provider co-location, or a corridor statistic alone cannot pass DR-4.
