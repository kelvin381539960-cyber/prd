## Evidence interpretation

- **Supports** states the bounded claim that the source can carry.
- **Does Not Support** states the claims that must not be inferred from it.
- **Claim type** distinguishes provider-reported, survey, network/model, academic, macro, or category evidence.
- **Evidence strength** uses Strong / Medium / Low / Unknown and is claim-specific, not a total score.

## A — Deel 2026 Global Hiring Report

- **Supports:** 2025 dataset context of more than 1,000,000 active worker contracts, 37,000+ companies, and 150+ countries; high-inflation-market contractor preference for USD or stablecoin; Argentina’s 84.6% USD-versus-local-currency preference; stablecoin contractor adoption ranking of Argentina, Cameroon, South Korea, Türkiye, Vietnam, and Tajikistan as a regional co-location signal.
- **Does Not Support:** USD preference equals stablecoin preference; a global stablecoin-user count; a same-user S1 ∩ S2 longitudinal cohort; retained balances; AIX reachability or right-to-win.
- **Claim type:** Provider-reported labor-market and compensation evidence; directional regional co-location.
- **Evidence strength:** Medium for contractor context and co-location; Low for same-user overlap, retention, and target sizing.
- **URL:** https://www.deel.com/global-hiring-report-2026/

## B — BVNK / YouGov Stablecoin Utility 2026

- **Supports:** In a 4,658-person, 15-country crypto-engaged cohort, 39% report getting paid in stablecoins; stablecoin is about 35% of reported annual earnings for the relevant cohort; 27% report everyday activities; average wallet is about $200; 28% convert or spend within days; 71% are interested in a linked card; 77% would open a stablecoin wallet with a primary bank or fintech; stablecoin holding is reported at 60% for emerging crypto-engaged respondents, 79% in Africa, and 45% in high-income markets.
- **Does Not Support:** General-population penetration; a clean salary or contractor denominator; professional-only income because the income signal mixes professional and family/friends contexts; same-user overlap with FX savers; retention across pay cycles; AIX market size or right-to-win.
- **Claim type:** Self-reported survey user evidence in a crypto-engaged/current-former-intended crypto cohort.
- **Evidence strength:** Medium for behavior signals inside the cohort; Low for global prevalence, worker-only behavior, retention, and unique-user sizing.
- **URL:** https://www.bvnk.com/utility
